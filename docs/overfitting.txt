Overfitting occurs when a machine learning model learns the training data too well, including its noise and outliers, resulting in poor performance on new, unseen data. Signs of overfitting include very high training accuracy but low validation accuracy. Common solutions include regularization (L1/L2), dropout in neural networks, cross-validation, reducing model complexity, and gathering more training data. The opposite problem, underfitting, happens when a model is too simple to capture the underlying pattern in the data.
